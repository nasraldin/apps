# Money – Bank Tracker

A private money tracker for UAE bank accounts, cards and spending. Your data stays on your iPhone.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy/)
- [Support](support/)

**Private by design.** No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics. Money reads only the bank messages that your own Shortcuts automation sends to it, and everything is processed and stored on your iPhone.

**What it does**

- Turns UAE bank SMS alerts (English and Arabic) into transactions on the right account
- Matches transfers between your own banks so they are never counted twice
- Checks your balance against the bank's balance in each message
- Records Apple Pay payments through the Wallet automation
- Budgets, savings goals, bills, subscriptions and insights
- Zakat with nisab, Hijri hawl, payments and history
- Widgets, reminders, Face ID lock and encrypted backups
- Full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout, Light and Dark mode
