# Money – Bank Tracker

[العربية](ar/)

Money keeps track of your UAE bank accounts, cards and spending, privately on your iPhone.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy/)
- [Support](support/)

## What it does

When your bank sends you a message about a payment, a transfer or your salary, Money turns it into a transaction on the right account. You set this up once with a Shortcuts automation, and after that it just works. Anything the app isn't sure about waits for you to check instead of being guessed.

- Works with English and Arabic messages from UAE banks
- Spots transfers between your own accounts, so nothing is counted twice
- Compares your balance with the one in each bank message
- Records Apple Pay payments, too
- Budgets, savings goals, bills, subscriptions and spending insights
- Zakat: nisab, the Hijri year (hawl), payments and history
- Widgets, reminders, Face ID lock and encrypted backups
- Full Arabic interface, plus Light and Dark mode

## Private by design

There's no account to create, no server, no ads and no analytics. Money only reads the messages that your own automation sends it, and everything stays on your iPhone.
