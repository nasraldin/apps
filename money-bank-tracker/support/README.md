## Getting started

1. Add your accounts with their current balances. Include the last 4 digits that appear in your bank's messages, so Money can match them to the right account.
2. In the app, open **Settings › Bank messages** and follow the steps to create the Shortcuts automation that sends your bank messages to Money.
3. If you like, set up Apple Pay recording too, from **Settings › Apple Wallet**.

## Common questions

**Does Money read my messages?**
No. It only receives the messages your own Shortcuts automation sends to it.

**Why did a message go to Review?**
Money wasn't sure which account it belongs to, or what kind of transaction it is. Approve it or correct it once, and the app remembers the merchant next time.

**What does "Transfers waiting" mean?**
Your bank said money left your account but didn't say where it went. Open it from Home, then choose **Sent to someone** or pick the account it arrived in.

**My balance doesn't match my bank.**
Open the account, tap **Reconcile** and enter the balance from your bank's app.

**How do I move to a new iPhone?**
Go to **Settings › Backup, import & export**, create a backup and save the file. On your new iPhone, restore it using the same password.
