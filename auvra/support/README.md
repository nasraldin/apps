# Support

Auvra – Gold Prices & Markets · [العربية](ar/)

## Getting started

1. Choose your home market and the markets you follow during onboarding. You can change these later in Settings.
2. Use Home for your chosen markets and currencies, Markets for local estimates, and Currencies for gold prices and dollar exchange rates.
3. Open Compare or Calculator from Home. To add a widget, follow **Settings › Widgets**.

## Common questions

**Are these gold shop prices?**
No. Local prices marked **Spot-equivalent** are calculated from the international spot price, exchange rate and gold purity. Shops add making charges, margins and taxes. Retail and dealer prices are not shown without a licensed source.

**Where does the data come from?**
International spot prices and price history come from [gold-api.com](https://gold-api.com). Current exchange rates are provided by [Exchangerate.dev](https://exchangerate.dev) and cached by the Auvra proxy, which refreshes them about every 15 minutes. The UAE dirham and Saudi riyal use their official US dollar pegs. Historical exchange rates come from [currency-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/currency-api), licensed under CC0-1.0. Source details are also available in Settings › Market Data.

**Why does a price say Market closed, Stale or Offline?**
International gold trading closes over the weekend. Auvra shows the last available quote and its update time when markets are closed or a connection is unavailable. Stale means the data is older than expected; it is not a new quote. If no saved data is available, the app shows an unavailable state.

**Are widgets live?**
No. Widgets refresh roughly every 15 minutes when iOS allows, and may refresh less often while markets are closed. Each entry shows its update time. Open the app to check the latest available quote.

**How do I remove my saved data?**
Open **Settings › Privacy & Storage › Clear All App Data**. This removes your saved preferences, favorites and cached market data.
