# Auvra – Gold Prices & Markets

[العربية](ar/)

Auvra shows the international gold price alongside local estimates for the UAE, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Every price shows its type and when it was last updated.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy/)
- [Support](support/)
- [Auvra website](https://nasraldin.github.io/auvra-site/)

## What it does

- International spot price in US dollars, with price history
- Local estimates by karat, currency and weight unit
- Market comparisons on the same purity, unit and currency
- Gold prices and US dollar exchange rates in dozens of currencies
- A calculator for weight, karat, premium, making charges and VAT
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with an update time on every entry
- English and Arabic, Light and Dark appearance, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver

## What the prices mean

Local prices marked **Spot-equivalent** are calculated from the international spot price, exchange rates and gold purity. They are not shop prices. Retail and dealer quotes are not shown without a licensed source. Widgets update when iOS allows and are not live tickers.

Prices are for information only and are not investment advice.

## Privacy

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Preferences and favorites stay on your iPhone. The app connects to market-data services to retrieve prices and charts; the [privacy policy](privacy/) explains these requests.
