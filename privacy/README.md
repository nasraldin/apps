# Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026 · [العربية](ar/)

This policy covers every app I make that doesn't have a privacy page of its own. If an app has its own page, that page is the one that applies.

> **The short version:** my apps don't collect your data. What you put into them stays on your device.

## What I collect

Nothing. My apps don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and I never sell or share information about you.

## Where your data lives

On your device, in the app's own storage. If an app offers backups or sync, they only go where you choose to send them, and the app's own page explains how.

## Permissions

An app asks for a permission, such as Face ID, contacts or notifications, only when a feature needs it, and the request tells you why. You can change any permission at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone.

## Deleting your data

Deleting an app removes its data from your device. Apps that keep more than a few settings also let you erase everything from inside the app.

## Children

My apps aren't made for children, and they don't collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If anything changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
